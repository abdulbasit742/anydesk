# Sector 171: AI in Education (EdTech) & E-Learning (US 2026 Directory)

This document provides a comprehensive A-to-Z directory of top US-based startups, companies, and platforms operating at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Education Technology (EdTech), E-Learning, and Adaptive Tutoring for 2026. ## Executive Summary & Sector Overview

The education technology and e-learning sectors are undergoing a transformative shift in 2026. Leveraging generative AI personal tutors, automated grading and assessment engines, intelligent content generation, and immersive virtual simulations, modern edtech platforms rely heavily on scalable machine learning infrastructure and robust API integrations.

Remote project outreach targeting these innovators focuses on supplying expert API integration, secure student data privacy pipelines, custom LLM fine-tuning for educational curricula, and scalable cloud-native architectures.

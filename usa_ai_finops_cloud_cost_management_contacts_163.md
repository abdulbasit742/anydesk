The FinOps, cloud cost management, and IT infrastructure optimization sectors are experiencing unprecedented AI-driven demand in 2026. As enterprises scale resource-intensive generative AI models and multi-cloud architectures, managing token-level costs, automating cloud commitments, optimizing Kubernetes clusters, and eliminating infrastructure waste have become mission-critical.

## Executive Summary & Sector Overview

The aerospace, space exploration, and satellite telemetry sectors are undergoing an AI-driven revolution in 2026. With the proliferation of commercial mega-constellations, AI-powered space situational awareness (SSA), autonomous spacecraft navigation, in-space manufacturing, and rapid satellite bus production, US-based space tech innovators are scaling aggressively.

Remote project outreach targeting these innovators focuses on supplying expert API integration, secure low-latency telemetry processing pipelines, custom computer vision fine-tuning for orbital imagery, and robust scalable cloud architectures for mission control software.
